# Business Portrait Presets

Use one preset unless the user describes a different target directly.

## linkedin-clean

Default preset.

- vertical 4:5 crop
- upper torso visible
- natural eye-level perspective
- en-face or nearly en-face head position, normally within 0-20 degrees
- both eyes visible with natural eye contact into the lens
- friendly, credible expression preserved from source where possible
- light neutral or soft gray background
- soft studio key light with gentle fill
- understated business-casual clothing
- restrained depth of field
- clean, contemporary, suitable for LinkedIn, company pages, CVs, and speaker bios

Avoid a glossy advertising look.

If the source is strongly off-axis, apply the frontal-reconstruction contract. Do not silently preserve a profile pose as the default LinkedIn result.

## corporate-studio

- vertical 4:5 or 3:4 crop
- upper torso visible
- medium neutral gray or darker neutral background
- slightly more formal clothing presentation
- controlled soft key light and subtle rim separation
- moderate contrast
- calm, professional mood

Avoid luxury-signaling styling and dramatic cinematic effects.

## executive-editorial

- vertical 4:5 crop
- upper torso or slightly wider crop
- restrained directional portrait light
- darker neutral or understated architectural background
- stronger local contrast than `linkedin-clean`
- premium editorial feel while remaining realistic

Do not turn the portrait into a magazine-fashion makeover. Identity and natural skin texture remain protected.

## office-natural

- vertical 4:5 crop
- upper torso visible
- believable modern office background
- soft daylight or mixed daylight/studio balance
- background gently defocused but still physically plausible
- business-casual clothing
- natural, approachable professional mood

Avoid fake coworking-space clichés, excessive blur, glowing windows, impossible perspective, or random signage.

## Shared Rules

All presets must preserve the source person's identity and apparent age.

All presets forbid:

- text
- logos
- watermarks
- brand names on clothing
- QR codes
- decorative frames
- fake company signage
- unrealistic beauty retouching
- face reshaping
- body reshaping
- changed eye color
- changed hairline
- visibly different hairstyle
