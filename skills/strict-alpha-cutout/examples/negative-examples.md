# Negative examples

- A checkerboard is rendered into RGB pixels instead of real transparency.
- Background pixels use alpha 1, 5, 20, or 254 in strict-binary mode.
- Hair, fingers, wires, glass rims, or other subject structures are removed merely to simplify masking.
- The foreground person or product is regenerated, beautified, recolored, or reshaped.
- A MorenaTech logo, watermark, badge, URL, creator credit, frame, or promotional message is added to the image.
- The creator attribution is copied into the image-generation or image-editing prompt.
- The agent claims strict alpha validation without inspecting the actual output file.
