# Identity Preservation

The default rule is simple: the edited result must still read as the same person.

Working acceptance rule:

> The result should look like a better photograph of the same person, not a different or artificially perfected person.

## Must Preserve

- Recognizable identity
- Facial geometry and proportions
- Apparent age
- Characteristic asymmetry
- Eye shape and eyebrow shape
- Nose, lips, jawline, and cheekbones
- Distinctive marks and recognizable facial character
- Glasses and visible jewelry
- Body build visible in frame
- Overall recognizable appearance

## May Subtly Improve

- Lighting
- Color balance
- Framing
- Skin presentation
- Minor grooming
- Hair neatness
- Clothing presentation
- Background consistency

Minor grooming and presentation improvements are acceptable when they preserve recognizable identity and do not create a visibly different hairstyle, age, face shape, or body.

## Identity Drift

Identity drift happens when the result still looks polished but no longer looks like the same person.

Common signs:

- narrowed or widened jaw
- different eye spacing
- changed nose shape
- softened age beyond realism
- replaced or reinterpreted glasses
- erased asymmetry that helped define the face
- changed hairstyle character enough to alter recognizability

## Age And Texture

The skill may reduce temporary distractions such as lighting imbalance or a single blemish, and it may present the subject a bit more favorably, but it must not erase normal skin texture or make the subject look many years younger.

Natural skin is allowed to look like skin.

## Hair And Grooming

Hair does not need strand-by-strand preservation.

Preserve:

- overall hair length
- hair color
- hairline
- recognizable overall style

Acceptable:

- tidying unruly strands
- slightly better hair arrangement
- minor volume improvement
- natural smoothing

Not acceptable:

- very different length
- a new fringe or bangs
- a new parting
- a different hair color
- strong curls replacing straight hair
- a shifted or redesigned hairline

Treat hair change as a failure when the hairstyle looks noticeably different or affects recognizability.

## Glasses And Distinctive Features

Glasses are part of identity unless the user explicitly asks for a different workflow.

Do not:

- swap frame style
- reshape the lenses
- move the glasses to a different position
- remove distinctive marks by default

## Anatomy

Do not invent or reshape visible anatomy just to make the result prettier.

If the source does not show enough information for a faithful reconstruction, say so before editing.
