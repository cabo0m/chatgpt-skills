# Privacy And Rights

## Repository Rules

- Do not place user photos in this repository.
- Do not place user photos in tests, examples, README, or promotional materials.
- Do not describe a private photo in a way that identifies the person.

## Public Corpus Rules

- Do not use Facebook, Instagram, or similar social-media images as public corpus material without explicit rights and permission.
- Prefer text-only or synthetic examples in the package.

## Script Behavior

- Scripts in this package must be read-only or deterministic validation helpers.
- Scripts must not upload images.
- Scripts must not collect personal data.

## Rights Reminder

Having access to an image in a conversation is not the same as having publication rights.
