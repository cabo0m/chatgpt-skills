# Packaging And Text Preservation

Retail packaging contains factual information.

## Preserve

- brand spelling
- product name
- quantity
- dimensions
- connector standard
- warranty claims
- certification marks
- barcodes / QR codes as source pixels
- legal marks
- printed color blocks
- package die-cut / hanger geometry

## Never infer unreadable text

If source text is blurred, blocked, cropped, or too small:

- do not rewrite it from product knowledge
- do not search the web and replace it
- do not sharpen it into invented characters
- preserve the source ambiguity

## Perspective correction

Perspective may be corrected when it is a geometric transformation of the source.

Do not re-typeset the package after straightening.

## Codes

A regenerated barcode or QR code is not equivalent to the original, even if it looks plausible.

Never claim an invented code is preserved.
