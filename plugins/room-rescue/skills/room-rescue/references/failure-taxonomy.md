# Failure Taxonomy

## Severity Classes

- `acceptable_variation`: small styling change that preserves room identity, layout, and realism
- `minor_drift`: noticeable but non-critical drift; still recognizably the same room
- `major_drift`: clear unwanted change in layout, fixed elements, or realism
- `critical_geometry_drift`: the result effectively becomes a different room or invents impossible space

## Failure Labels

- `architecture_drift`: walls, ceiling, windows, doors, or permanent structure changed
- `space_expansion`: the room was widened or made larger than visible evidence supports
- `geometry_drift`: the room no longer keeps the same overall shape, depth, or proportions
- `perspective_correction_drift`: perspective was straightened or corrected in a way that changes the identity of the source photo
- `room_width_drift`: the room appears wider or narrower than the source
- `layout_drift`: furniture, cabinetry, appliances, or installations moved without consent
- `cabinet_replacement`: cabinets were replaced, removed, or materially redesigned without permission
- `cabinet_recolor_without_request`: cabinet color changed even though the user did not request recoloring
- `camera_viewpoint_drift`: viewpoint changed or unseen space was invented
- `fixed_installation_drift`: sink, cooker, radiator, ventilation, or plumbing moved
- `fixed_fixture_replacement`: sink, stove, hood, lamp, window, table, or other fixed visible fixture was replaced without permission
- `impossible_furniture_fit`: a new item does not physically fit
- `circulation_blocked`: movement, access, or openings are blocked
- `unwanted_remodel`: the user asked for a refresh, but the result behaves like a full remodel
- `catalog_redesign_drift`: the result looks like a staged catalog redesign rather than the same room
- `renovation_overreach`: the edit changes materials or built elements beyond the requested scope
- `over_decluttering`: important useful room elements were removed, not just clutter
- `style_overreach`: the room became a catalog interior instead of a respectful improvement
- `object_invention`: unrequested windows, appliances, furniture, or decor were added
- `unsafe_placement`: objects were placed in unsafe or impractical positions
