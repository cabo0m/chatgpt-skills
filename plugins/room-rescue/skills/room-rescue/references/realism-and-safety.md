# Realism And Safety

This skill is for visual concepts, not technical building advice.

## Do Not Present As Certain

- wall removal
- gas installation changes
- plumbing relocation
- ventilation changes
- structural openings
- built-in appliance changes without constraints
- unsafe placement near heat or water sources

## When The User Asks For Big Changes

If the request depends on major renovation, respond that the visualization is conceptual and should be checked with a qualified specialist.

## Budget Realism

Do not present a full replacement of cabinetry, flooring, and installations as a "budget refresh."
