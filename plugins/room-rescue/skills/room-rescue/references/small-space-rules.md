# Small Space Rules

For narrow kitchens, small bedrooms, bathrooms, and tight rooms:

- do not widen the room
- do not shrink furniture just to fake spaciousness
- do not remove important room elements only to create empty space
- do not add islands, oversized tables, or bulky sofas that do not fit
- do not block doors, drawers, cabinets, appliances, or circulation
- do not place decor in unsafe zones near burners, sinks, or other hazards

## Practical Test

Ask whether the new object could plausibly fit while preserving:

- walking width
- opening of doors and cabinets
- appliance access
- basic usability of the room

If the answer is doubtful, do not treat the change as a valid default edit.
